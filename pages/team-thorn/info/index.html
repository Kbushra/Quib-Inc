Team Thorn is a recent organisation that members of Quib Inc. has joined, working mainly on Floweytale.

Team Thorn is a relatively large indie team, with a variety of talented artists, musicians and programmers.

Their main project is Floweytale, a take on the RPG Undertale that acts as a prequel to the game. It follows the plot of Flowey, once the prince of the kingdom, as they slowly turn from a scared, lonely flower to a masochistic murderer who kills just to see what happens. Through their power of Determination, they cannot die, and so as the days pass they become more curious as to what they can inflict upon the world until their morality is gone. Only current work available for show as of now are small sneak-peaks into Floweytale.
