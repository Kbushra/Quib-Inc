Mediaocre Games

Quib Inc. collaborates with Mediaocre Games on larger team projects, including original concepts and indie fangames.

The team includes Krystal, Jayden, Ava and Aaqib. Together we focus on game development, art direction and interactive storytelling.

Our main project is Undertem, a take on the RPG Undertale where the Temmies revolt against monsterkind after oppression, and turn the Underground into a dictatorship.
The team for Undertem is all of Mediaocre, as well as the pixel artists Krispy (@kr_spy), Sha (@youk_owjak), Skater (@skaterfc), and the promotional/high res artist Bendy (@mrbendyfeathers).
Undertem currently has a Ruins theme and the entirety of the Ruins is coded. It is currently going through a rewrite, however, with the goal of balancing out politics and humour, as well as keeping the current events mostly similar.
